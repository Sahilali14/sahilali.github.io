Roboweeder Solo — Lightweight Single-Bay Robotic Weeder

Developed a lightweight single-bay version of the existing three-bay Roboweeder to create a more compact solution for the Indian and Australian markets. The objective was to reduce machine size and weight while integrating the required tanks, camera system, electronics and manifold mechanism without compromising functionality.

I owned the mechanical design from concept development through manufacturing, including the overall mechanical architecture, structural design and key engineering decisions.

Engineering Focus

Compact machine architecture, welded structural design, adjustable camera mounting, environmental protection, GD&T and tolerancing, and prototype-driven design iteration.

3 → 1 Bay Compact single-bay version of the three-bay Roboweeder
India & Australia Target markets for the lightweight platform

Engineering Challenge

The existing Roboweeder uses a three-bay configuration. A smaller machine was needed, so the tanks, camera system, electronics and manifold mechanism all had to be packaged into a single, lighter bay without compromising functionality.

My Contribution

  • Mechanical ownership: Owned the mechanical design from concept development through manufacturing, including the overall architecture, structural design and key engineering decisions.
  • Requirements: Worked with cross-functional teams to translate product requirements into mechanical design requirements.
  • Concept development: Developed the single-bay machine concept with integrated tanks and a horizontally adjustable, sliding camera mounting system.
  • Complete machine structure: Designed the chassis, structural framework, skirting, camera mounts, hood, hitch points and the manifold up/down mechanism.
  • Fits, clearances and tolerances: Defined fits, clearances and tolerances for individual components and assemblies to ensure proper functionality, manufacturability and assembly.
  • Welded structures and drawings: Developed complex welded structures and manufacturing-ready drawings with GD&T and appropriate tolerancing. Worked with other engineers who supported drawing creation, while maintaining ownership of the design intent and critical engineering requirements.
  • Skirting system: Designed the skirting to protect solenoids and internal components from wind and external environmental conditions.
  • Hood and electronics mounting: Designed the hood and the mounting provisions for PDUs and other electronic components.
  • Prototype and iteration: Developed the initial prototype and incorporated feedback from prototype builds and assembly into subsequent design iterations.

Impact

Developed the mechanical architecture and initial prototype of a lightweight single-bay Roboweeder platform. The project involved system-level mechanical design, structural engineering, GD&T, tolerance definition, prototyping, manufacturing coordination and cross-functional problem solving from concept through manufacturing.
