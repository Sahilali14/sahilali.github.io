RTK Base Station — RF Optimization & IP67 Enclosure Design

Redesigned an RTK Base Station to address communication and signal-range issues caused by the existing antenna and enclosure configuration. The objective was to improve RF transparency while achieving IP67 environmental protection and maintaining mechanical functionality.

I owned the mechanical development from concept through mass production, including internal component sizing, standard enclosure selection, mechanical packaging, tolerance optimization, and production readiness for the US launch of the Roboweeder product.

Engineering Focus

Compact electronics packaging, RF-aware mechanical design, antenna integration, environmental sealing, and precision assembly.

IP67 Required ingress protection successfully achieved
±0.5 mm LED and enclosure positioning in the initial design iteration

Engineering Challenge

The existing antenna and enclosure configuration affected communication performance and signal range. The redesign needed to improve RF transparency while accommodating all internal electronics and mechanical components within a compact enclosure and maintaining the required environmental protection.

My Contribution

  • Internal component sizing: Estimated the dimensions and space requirements of the internal electronics and mechanical components to establish the packaging envelope and identify fitment constraints.
  • Standard enclosure selection: Evaluated and selected a standard Takachi enclosure based on the available space, component packaging, and product requirements.
  • Mechanical packaging: Designed the internal arrangement, component interfaces, and mechanical details around the selected enclosure to accommodate the electronics and mechanical components within the available space.
  • RF optimization: Evaluated antenna placement, enclosure configuration, and surrounding material selection to improve RF transparency and address communication and signal-range issues.
  • Tolerance stack-up: Analyzed critical assembly interfaces and achieved LED and enclosure positioning within ±0.5 mm during the initial design iteration.
  • IP67 validation: Successfully achieved the required IP67 protection while maintaining mechanical functionality and the required communication performance.
  • Assembly optimization: Worked closely with the assembly team to identify and resolve fitment and assembly issues through subsequent design revisions.
  • Production and launch: Released the final design within the required timeline to support the US launch of the Roboweeder product.

Design Approach

  • Estimated component sizes and established the internal packaging requirements before finalizing the mechanical arrangement.
  • Selected a standard Takachi enclosure and developed the internal mechanical layout around its available dimensions.
  • Considered antenna placement and surrounding materials as part of the RF-aware enclosure design.
  • Applied tolerance analysis to critical interfaces and refined component fitment through assembly feedback.
  • Progressed the design through validation and production release within the product-launch timeline.

Impact

Delivered a production-ready RTK Base Station design that addressed RF transparency and communication challenges while achieving IP67 protection. Early component sizing, standard Takachi enclosure selection, and internal packaging helped establish the mechanical architecture. Positioning accuracy within ±0.5 mm in the initial iteration and collaboration with the assembly team supported design refinement and the US product launch.
