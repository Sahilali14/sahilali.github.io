RTK Base Station — RF Optimization & IP67 Enclosure Design

Optimized the mechanical enclosure and component positioning of an RTK base station used in an agricultural robotics platform. The project focused on addressing communication performance challenges while maintaining environmental protection, dimensional accuracy, and manufacturing feasibility.

Engineering Focus

RF-aware enclosure design, antenna integration, IP67 protection, component positioning, and production-ready mechanical engineering.

IP67 Ingress protection achieved
±0.5 mm LED positioning tolerance in the first iteration

Engineering Challenge

The existing arrangement of the antenna and enclosure components affected communication performance and signal range. The design needed to balance RF transparency, antenna placement, environmental sealing, and accurate positioning of the external components.

My Contribution

  • Evaluated enclosure material and component placement requirements to support RF signal transmission.
  • Optimized the mechanical arrangement of the antenna, enclosure, and LED to address communication and packaging constraints.
  • Developed the enclosure design to achieve the required IP67 environmental protection.
  • Applied tolerance stack-up considerations to control LED positioning within ±0.5 mm during the first design iteration.
  • Considered sealing, assembly, and manufacturing requirements during mechanical design development.
  • Coordinated design refinement and production requirements to support a smooth transition from validation to mass production.

Design & Validation Approach

  • Reviewed the relationship between antenna location, enclosure material, and communication performance.
  • Refined enclosure geometry and component interfaces around environmental sealing requirements.
  • Applied dimensional control and tolerance analysis to achieve the required component positioning.
  • Validated the enclosure against the required IP67 protection level and progressed the design toward production.

Impact

Delivered a production-ready RTK base station enclosure design that addressed RF integration challenges while achieving IP67 protection. Accurate LED positioning within ±0.5 mm in the first iteration, together with mechanical design optimization and manufacturing coordination, supported the transition from concept to mass production.
