IP69K-Rated Camera Enclosure

Mechanical Design | Sealing Engineering | Product Validation

Project Overview

Designed and developed a sealed camera enclosure to accommodate updated electronics and meet demanding ingress-protection requirements.

Engineering Challenge

The existing camera design required improved ingress protection while accommodating changes to the PCB and electronics packaging.

Design Approach

  • Developed the mechanical enclosure and sealing features.
  • Designed a test block to evaluate the housing seal.
  • Coordinated machining and manufacturing activities.
  • Supported prototype evaluation and ingress-protection testing.

Validation and Outcome

IP67 testing was conducted in-house, and IP69K laboratory validation was achieved.

My Contribution

Mechanical enclosure design, sealing-interface development, prototype evaluation, and manufacturing coordination.
