IP69K-Rated Camera Enclosure

Redesigned the Niqo Sense Camera to accommodate updated electronic components, improve assembly, and achieve the required environmental protection. The existing camera was not IP-rated, creating a significant sealing and packaging challenge.

What I Did

  • Conducted waterproofing experiments on the existing camera to identify leakage paths and establish design requirements.
  • Worked with cross-functional teams to define mechanical, electrical, sealing, and assembly requirements.
  • Designed and validated a dovetail-groove housing concept as an initial platform for waterproofing testing.
  • Designed the Niqo Sense Camera enclosure with integrated sealing features while accommodating updated PCB and electronic components.
  • Worked closely with the assembly team to incorporate fitment feedback and optimize the design with fewer iterations.
  • Coordinated with external machining vendors for rapid prototype parts and quick design iterations.
  • Released the final design within a tight development timeline and successfully achieved IP69K rating through laboratory testing.

Impact

Successfully developed an IP69K-rated camera from a previously non-IP-rated design while accommodating updated electronics and improving assembly. Rapid prototyping, in-house IP67 testing, iterative design optimization, and close collaboration with cross-functional teams played a key role in achieving the required performance within the target timeline.
