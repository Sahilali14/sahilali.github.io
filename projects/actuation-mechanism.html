Actuation Mechanism for Live Manifold Control

The actuation mechanism shown on its own, without the host machine.

Developed a live manifold actuation mechanism for the three-bay Roboweeder to maintain a consistent ground-to-nozzle distance while operating over uneven agricultural terrain.

The mechanism is an interface that goes onto both the Robo Thinner and the Roboweeder products.

Engineering Focus

Linear actuation, mechanical calculations, sensor integration, contamination protection, sheet-metal design, and precision machining tolerances.

1 mm Shaft-to-shaft alignment tolerance achieved during machining
1st iteration Required functionality achieved in the first design iteration

Engineering Challenge

Agricultural terrain is uneven, so the distance between the ground and the spray nozzles changes as the machine moves. The manifold position needed to be adjusted live to keep the nozzle height consistent and maintain spray performance, while keeping the moving parts protected from agricultural contamination.

My Contribution

  • Mechanism and calculations: Performed mechanical calculations and developed the linear actuator-based mechanism.
  • Actuator selection: Selected the LINAK linear actuator based on load, stroke and operating requirements.
  • Depth sensing: Integrated a laser-based depth sensing system to monitor the ground-to-nozzle distance.
  • Structural modification: Modified the Roboweeder structure to accommodate the actuator, linear guides, bushings and manifold mechanism.
  • Mounting and protection: Designed sheet-metal mounting components and shaft bellows to protect moving components from agricultural contamination.
  • Drawings: Developed detailed welding and manufacturing drawings.
  • Fit checks and tolerances: Performed fit checks and tolerance analysis, achieving 1 mm shaft-to-shaft alignment tolerance during machining.
  • First-iteration success: Achieved the required functionality with the first design iteration.

Impact

Enabled live adjustment of the manifold position to maintain consistent nozzle height and improve spray performance over uneven terrain.
