Burrgon M — Automated Gear Deburring Machine

Developed a mechanical gear deburring machine to automate the removal of machining burrs from gear cutting edges. The manual process was time-consuming and limited production throughput.

I worked directly with the CEO of Hydax and contributed across the complete product development lifecycle, from concept design and detailed engineering to manufacturing, assembly, testing and validation.

Engineering Focus

Machine design, pneumatic tool feed, tool buffing, motor-driven workpiece rotation, and end-to-end product development.

4 months From concept to a functional machine
AMTEX 2022 Machine presented to prospective customers in New Delhi

My Contribution

  • Mechanical design and development: Led the mechanical design and supported end-to-end machine development, from concept to manufacturing.
  • Pneumatic tool-feed mechanism: Designed the pneumatic mechanism that feeds the deburring tool to the gear.
  • Tool buffing mechanism: Designed the buffing mechanism used in the deburring process.
  • Workpiece rotation: Designed a motor-driven gearbox system for rotating the workpiece.
  • Manufacturing and assembly: Coordinated with external manufacturing vendors and collaborated with the assembly team to ensure smooth machine integration.
  • Testing and validation: Performed machine testing and validation to verify functionality.
  • Customer presentation: Presented the machine to prospective customers at AMTEX 2022, New Delhi.

Impact

Reduced manual deburring requirements and improved the production workflow. The machine was delivered from concept to a functional solution within four months.
