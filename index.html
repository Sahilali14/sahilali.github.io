IP69K-Rated Camera Enclosure

Redesigned the Niqo Sense Camera to accommodate updated electronic components, improve assembly, and achieve the required environmental protection. The existing camera was not IP-rated, creating a significant sealing and packaging challenge.

Key contributions: Waterproofing experiments, dovetail-groove sealing concept, enclosure design with integrated sealing features, vendor coordination, and rapid prototyping.

Result: Took a previously non-IP-rated design to a laboratory-validated IP69K rating within a tight development timeline.

RTK Base Station — RF Optimization & IP67 Enclosure Design

Optimized the mechanical enclosure and component positioning of an RTK base station for an agricultural robotics platform, addressing communication performance while maintaining environmental protection and manufacturing feasibility.

Key contributions: RF-aware enclosure design, antenna placement and material evaluation, IP67 sealing, tolerance stack-up, LED positioning control, and manufacturing coordination.

Result: Achieved IP67 protection, controlled LED positioning to ±0.5 mm during the first design iteration, and progressed the design from concept through validation to mass production.
