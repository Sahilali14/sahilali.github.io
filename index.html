Roboweeder Solo — Lightweight Single-Bay Robotic Weeder

Developed a lightweight single-bay version of the three-bay Roboweeder, creating a more compact machine for the Indian and Australian markets while integrating the tanks, camera system, electronics and manifold mechanism.

Key contributions: Mechanical architecture, chassis and structural framework, sliding camera mounting system, skirting, hood, manifold up/down mechanism, welded structures, GD&T drawings, and prototype development.

MECHANICAL DESIGN · ACTUATION · SHEET METAL · PRECISION

Actuation Mechanism for Live Manifold Control

Developed a live manifold actuation mechanism for the three-bay Roboweeder to keep a consistent ground-to-nozzle distance while operating over uneven agricultural terrain. The interface is used on both the Robo Thinner and Roboweeder products.

Key contributions: Mechanical calculations, LINAK linear actuator selection, laser depth-sensing integration, linear guides and bushings, sheet-metal mounts, shaft bellows, welding and manufacturing drawings, and tolerance analysis.

Result: Achieved the required functionality in the first design iteration, with 1 mm shaft-to-shaft alignment tolerance during machining, enabling live manifold height adjustment.

MECHANICAL DESIGN · SEALING · PRODUCT VALIDATION

IP69K-Rated Camera Enclosure

Redesigned the Niqo Sense Camera to accommodate updated electronic components, improve assembly, and achieve the required environmental protection. The existing camera was not IP-rated, creating a significant sealing and packaging challenge.

Key contributions: Waterproofing experiments, dovetail-groove sealing concept, enclosure design with integrated sealing features, vendor coordination, and rapid prototyping.

Result: Took a previously non-IP-rated design to a laboratory-validated IP69K rating within a tight development timeline.

MECHANICAL DESIGN · RF INTEGRATION · IP67 PROTECTION

RTK Base Station — RF Optimization & IP67 Enclosure Design

Redesigned an RTK Base Station to address communication and signal-range issues. Selected a standard Takachi enclosure and designed the internal mechanical packaging around the electronics and mechanical components.

Key contributions: Internal component sizing, Takachi enclosure selection, electronics packaging, RF-aware material and antenna placement, IP67 protection, tolerance stack-up, and assembly optimization.

Result: Achieved IP67 protection and LED/enclosure positioning within ±0.5 mm during the initial design iteration. Progressed the design from concept through mass production to support the US product launch.

MECHANICAL DESIGN · AUTOMATION · QUALITY CONTROL

Strip Qualifier — Automated QC System for Aztec Code Inspection

Designed an automated conveyor-based QC machine for Aztec code inspection, covering strip feeding, alignment, inspection and automatic rejection of failed strips.

Key contributions: Conveyor system design, adjustable camera, dome-light and sensor mounting, sorting mechanism, component sourcing, vendor coordination, and machine build support.

Result: Increased QC throughput from 4K to 15K strips/day (3.75×) and delivered a validated machine in 3 months, which was then replicated for three additional machines.

MECHANICAL DESIGN · AUTOMATION · PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Burrgon M — Automated Gear Deburring Machine

Developed a mechanical gear deburring machine to automate the removal of machining burrs from gear cutting edges, addressing a time-consuming manual process that limited production throughput.

Key contributions: Pneumatic tool-feed mechanism, tool buffing mechanism, motor-driven gearbox for workpiece rotation, vendor coordination, assembly support, and machine testing and validation.

Result: Reduced manual deburring and improved production workflow, delivering a functional machine from concept in four months and presenting it to prospective customers at AMTEX 2022, New Delhi.
